# Myoelectric prosthetic arm

A 3D-printed prosthetic arm whose servo motors are driven by muscle-signal sensing.

Convener project at the Tinkerers' Laboratory, IIT Bombay, Sep 2023 to Dec 2023. Team of 6 people.

## What I did

- Led the team of 6 people that built the arm.
- Designed a machine that turns PET bottles into 3D-printing filament, to cut the project's
  material cost.
